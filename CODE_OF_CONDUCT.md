# Community conduct

Keep discussions respectful and focused on the project. Describe behavior
and evidence, consider differing experience levels, and criticize ideas
without personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discrimination and publishing
another person's private information are not acceptable.

The maintainer may edit or remove disruptive content and restrict
participation to keep the project welcoming and useful. Use GitHub's report
abuse controls for conduct that violates its community guidelines. Keep
security disclosures on the private reporting channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
